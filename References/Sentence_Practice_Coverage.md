Paired sentences in 6γ and 6δ have separate cards labeled a/b. Exercise 6ξ has one card per translation direction in each pair. The two sentences in 7γ.1 also have separate cards. Other multi-sentence exercise items stay together.

Case and pronoun exercises retain the printed incomplete prompts; answers provide completed Greek and an English translation. English-to-Greek answers are suggested solutions, not a textbook answer key. For 6μ.6, both μετά + accusative (after) and μετά + genitive (with) are shown because the prompt alone permits either reading.

The category excludes vocabulary, form-changing drills (6ε/6ζ), exercises that only ask students to find words or forms in a reading (6α/6β/6κ/7α/7β), continuous reading passages, and grammar illustrations. The existing Translation category still includes reading and grammar sentence cards.

Thirty existing sentence cards retain their IDs and saved ratings. Four abbreviated 6η prompts now include the rest of their printed exercise item. The other eighty cards use new IDs c291–c370.

Run the coverage and interaction checks with `node --test tests/sentence-practice.test.cjs`.
